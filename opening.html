Research Directions

PixAIL works at the intersection of multimodal foundation models and agentic AI for medicine, spanning long-context reasoning, imaging–EHR fusion, and clinically grounded interactive agents.

LLMs, VLMs & Temporal Reasoning

  • Long-context multimodal reasoning across clinical text, imaging, video, structured EHR data, and longitudinal patient timelines.
  • Video-based foundation models for long-horizon spatiotemporal representation learning, temporal understanding, and fine-grained frame- and phase-level alignment.
  • Multimodal post-training and reasoning, including instruction tuning, preference optimization, RL-style alignment, and test-time reasoning.

Multimodal Foundation Models

  • Fusion across CT, MRI, ultrasound, and digital pathology with different resolutions, geometries, and acquisition protocols.
  • Integration of imaging with structured clinical, molecular, and genetic data for cross-modal diagnostic and clinical reasoning.
  • Calibration, uncertainty estimation, and robustness under distribution shift.

Multimodal Agents for Medicine

  • Interactive agents over video, imaging, clinical text, and patient records, with planning, retrieval, tool use, and auditable evidence chains.
  • Automated diagnosis, report generation, and clinical decision support, evaluated against clinician-derived ground truth.
  • Translation into clinical workflows, including human-AI collaboration, deployment constraints, safety, and failure modes such as automation bias.

If you are passionate about AI × Healthcare, algorithm and model innovation, multimodal learning and reasoning, and hope to apply methods to real clinical problems, welcome to join us!
